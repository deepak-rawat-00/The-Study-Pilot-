# The Study Pilot — Ethical Backlink Prospecting Database (100 Targets) & Outreach Manual

**Project**: The Study Pilot (https://thestudypilot.space)  
**Strategy**: 100% White-Hat, Value-First, Educational Resource Link Building  
**Strict Prohibition**: No link purchasing, no automated mass mailing, no private blog networks (PBNs), no reciprocal link rings, no deceptive link placement.

---

## Part 1: Outreach Rules of Engagement & Code of Ethics

1. **Manual Inspection Before Any Contact**:
   - Inspect the target page manually. Verify the coordinator or webmaster maintains active outbound links to third-party educational resources.
   - Ensure the recommended asset genuinely fills an unmet need on their specific page.
2. **Strictly Non-Automated Outreach**:
   - Every single email must be sent manually from a personal, legitimate email domain (e.g. `editorial@thestudypilot.space` or personal founder email).
   - Address the actual name of the resource coordinator, librarian, or director whenever discoverable.
3. **No Commercial or Transactional Demands**:
   - Never ask for paid placement. Never offer payment.
   - Emphasize that The Study Pilot's interactive tools and printable worksheets are 100% free, require zero user registration, contain no intrusive pop-ups, and respect user privacy through browser-local storage.
4. **Follow-Up Protocol**:
   - Maximum of **one** polite follow-up sent 7 to 10 days after the initial contact if no response is received.
   - If no reply is received after the single follow-up, mark the prospect as "Closed / Unresponsive" and never email again.
   - If any recipient asks to be removed or declines, immediately mark as "Do Not Contact".
5. **Natural Anchor Text**:
   - Request natural, descriptive anchor text (e.g., "The Study Pilot Study Planner Generator", "free printable study schedule worksheets", "interactive study hours calculator"). Avoid aggressive keyword-stuffed anchors.
